Inline message banner with a soft tinted fill — for confirmations, warnings and tips.

```jsx
<Alert tone="success" title="Solicitação recebida"
  icon={<i className="ph ph-check-circle" />}>
  A gente vai analisar o cenário e indicar o melhor caminho.
</Alert>
```

Tones: info/success/warning/danger. `title` optional, `icon` optional, `onClose` adds a dismiss button. Follow the TOTVS voice — orient and name the next step.
