Small pill for status, counts and categories.

```jsx
<Badge tone="success" dot>Ativo</Badge>
<Badge tone="cyan" emphasis="solid">Novo</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning">Pendente</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | cyan | purple | success | warning | danger`. `emphasis` = `soft` (default) or `solid`. `dot` adds a leading status dot. Keep labels to one or two words, sentence case.
